# Tool logo assets

The integration strip uses local copies of the brands' published artwork. Keep
their original colors and proportions. The business line uses an original SVG
with a solid white handset and call waves on the site's blue (`#0181F5`). Its
40 px rounded tile matches the visual weight of the surrounding app icons.

## Social icons

The footer, top bar and mobile menu use local Simple Icons SVGs for Facebook,
Instagram and LinkedIn, downloaded from the [Simple Icons CDN](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@latest/icons/).
They render in white on the site's dark backgrounds. Their shapes and brand
marks remain the respective owners' trademarks.
