## Option A — Human collaboration

Saved image: `a-human-collaboration.png`

### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: About Us website image concept, portrait 10:13 aspect ratio. One complete image, not a collage.
Primary request: Illustrate "We build simple systems that help cleaning businesses grow" and "a team that works with cleaning businesses to solve everyday challenges and build simpler ways to work."
Scene: a small, welcoming, sunlit workspace inside a modern cleaning business. A friendly female systems specialist in a plain navy overshirt sits beside a male cleaning business owner wearing a plain vibrant blue polo. Together they review a navy tablet on a warm oak table. The specialist gently points to the tablet; the owner looks engaged and relieved. Genuine comfortable collaboration, natural anatomy and hands, candid interaction rather than posing for the camera. The business owner has neatly swept brown hair; the specialist has medium-brown skin and loosely tied dark curly hair. An organized blue cleaning caddy is visible at the lower edge, anchoring this to a cleaning company.
Tablet: simple pale-blue interface with three large clear visual symbols for a customer message, calendar booking, and completed task, plus a small green check. No readable words, letters, numbers, tiny UI, or chart clutter.
Style: premium polished stylized 3D commercial illustration. Warm, friendly, grounded, professional adult characters with subtly rounded forms and expressive faces; convincing cloth texture and materials. Match the existing website hero's blue-uniformed brown-haired 3D cleaner, sunlit cream interior, warm wood, pale-blue technology visuals and optimistic professional mood. A coherent commercial 3D rendering, not a stock photo and not flat vector art.
Composition: vertical 10:13 portrait, medium view with both faces clearly visible in the upper third and the tablet interaction in the middle. Keep complete heads and working hands safely inside the frame. Balanced margins suitable for rounded portrait cropping, no built-in borders or frames, full-bleed environment.
Lighting/palette: beautiful soft daylight from a window on the left, ivory walls, light oak table, navy #010f34, vivid workwear blue #0181f5/#2077bc, tiny restrained green confirmation accent. Soft dimensional shadows and shallow depth of field.
Constraints: no headline, captions, typography, logos, watermarks, fake statistics, trophies, cash, robot, gears or holographic clutter. This is an illustration of service partnership, not a portrait of real team members. Opaque background.
